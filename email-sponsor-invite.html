The Syracuse Bakers Expo gathers artisan bakers, dessert innovators, and food enthusiasts to celebrate Central New York's vibrant culinary scene. By sponsoring the 2026 Expo, you invest in young creators, help vendors test new products, and ensure families across Syracuse have an uplifting place to gather.

Syracuse Bakers Expo 2026

March 26, 2026
5:30 PM – 9:00 PM

Finger Lakes Ballroom
Marriott Syracuse Downtown

 

Event Highlights

Guests cast votes for their favorite bakers while enjoying live entertainment, champagne, coffee, and tea.

Over 500 people attended in past years, and we expect 500–600 guests in 2026.

Youth Baking Labs & Mentorship: Budding chefs learn side-by-side with pros, gaining hands-on skills and confidence that keep Syracuse’s culinary scene thriving.

Awards & Recognition

  • Creativity & Design – $500
  • Texture & Taste – $500
  • Product Packaging – $250
  • Baker of the Year – $1,000 + special marketing promotion

Our Commitment to Local Talent

We uplift local talent and nurture the next generation of creators, especially the culinary arts, to inspire, heal, and connect Syracuse.

Why We Do What We Do

The Expo gives local bakers an affordable, visible stage and gives young talent a clear, inspiring way to step into the culinary world.

"Together, we can make Central New York a little bit sweeter."

Community In Focus

Bakers of every age step onto a professional stage, share their craft, and fuel Syracuse's creativity.

This year brings an exciting 14-week training program for community youth interested in baking, culminating in the chance to exhibit their work at the Expo.
